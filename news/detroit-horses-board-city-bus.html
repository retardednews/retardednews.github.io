Two Unusual Passengers Climb Aboard a Detroit City Bus. Their Handler Says It's Not Their First Time

The video went viral, the city launched an investigation, and the man behind it had a list of bus routes they had already tried.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 11, 2026 · Detroit, Michigan, USA · 2 min read

Just after 5 p.m. on November 7, a Detroit Department of Transportation bus pulled up at a stop near 7 Mile and Strasburg Street. The doors opened and two horses walked on.

Two men led them on board and then back off through the rear door. A woman named Ellessia Drew filmed the whole thing and posted it to Facebook, and it spread fast.

Not their first ride

One of the men was Jordan Williams, a co-founder of Urban City Cowboys, a group that teaches Detroit kids about horses and offers riding lessons and equestrian therapy for children with disabilities. He said he was heading home from a fundraiser and asked the driver if he could walk the horses through for a quick video.

He also said it wasn't a one-off. By his account, his horses had already been on buses on the Jefferson, Chalmers, Conners and 7 Mile routes. He joked that buses have got a lot smaller than they used to be, or that maybe he has got bigger.

The city was less amused. DDOT said the stunt went against its policy and could make for an unsafe and uncomfortable ride for passengers, and later released footage from the bus's own cameras. Officials said they had spoken to the driver.

"I didn't mean to get anybody in trouble," Williams said, according to LiveNOW from FOX.

There is one small loophole. DDOT does allow service animals, and under federal rules a miniature horse can count as one. The city said these two were clearly on board for other reasons. Williams' hope is that officials take it as a joke and come out to a rodeo.
