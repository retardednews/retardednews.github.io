Man Facing Car-Theft Charges Drives a Stolen Car to His Own Car-Theft Hearing

An auto theft task force watched him park. He never made it in front of the judge.

By RetardedNews Staff · March 25, 2026 · Salinas, California, USA · 2 min read

There are bad ideas, and then there is driving a stolen car to court for your stolen-car case.

A 41-year-old man from Soledad, California, was due to appear at the Monterey County courthouse in Salinas on a pending auto theft charge. At around 11 a.m. on Tuesday, March 24, 2026, he pulled into the courthouse parking area, on time and ready to face the music.

The car he arrived in had been reported stolen in San Jose.

Bad timing, worse audience

As luck would have it for everyone except him, members of a regional auto theft task force, known as MADCAT, were watching as he parked. Of all the parking lots in California, he had picked the one where car-theft specialists were literally on duty.

Deputies met him right outside the courtroom where his car-theft case was about to be heard.

He was arrested before he could set foot in front of the judge. The sheriff's office said the arrest went smoothly.

He was booked into Monterey County Jail on suspicion of unlawfully taking a vehicle, committing a felony while out on bail, and driving on a suspended licence. His original case is presumably now the least of his problems.
