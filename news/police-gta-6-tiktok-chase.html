Police Turn a Real Car Chase Into a GTA Video and Tell the Driver He Should Have Waited for GTA 6

Derbyshire officers added wanted stars, a minimap and a soundtrack. The driver gets to enjoy the game's launch from a cell.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 3, 2026 · Ripley, Derbyshire, England, UK · 3 min read

Police social media teams have discovered the most powerful weapon in law enforcement: video editing.

On November 3, Derbyshire Police posted a TikTok of officers chasing a black van, restyled to look like Grand Theft Auto. The clip came with a wanted-star display, a tiny map tracking the van and a dramatic soundtrack.

The caption suggested the driver could simply have waited a few more weeks and done his driving in GTA 6 instead.

Game over

Police said the 28-year-old driver was arrested for dangerous driving, driving while disqualified, drug-driving and possessing cannabis. The chase reportedly took place in Ripley.

Reports say he was jailed for 14 months, which means he will miss the real game's release.

Gamers spotted the HUD showed five wanted stars. GTA 6 has six.

Viewers being viewers, the main complaint online was not about the driving. It was about the star count, and whether the police got the game details right.
