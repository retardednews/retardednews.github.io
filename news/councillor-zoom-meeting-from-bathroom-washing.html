A Councillor Joins the Big Meeting on Zoom. The Mayor Has One Request: 'You Might Want to Move Your Camera'

Her colleagues could see exactly where she was calling from. So could 2,800 people watching the livestream. She did not see the problem.

By RetardedNews Staff · August 5, 2026 · Dunedin, Otago, New Zealand · 2 min read

Dunedin City Council's meeting on Monday, August 3, was a serious one. On the agenda was the New Zealand government's push to reorganise local councils, with possible mergers across the Otago region.

Councillor Jo Galer was overseas, so she dialled in by Zoom. When her video came up, a ripple of giggles went around the chamber.

She was in a bathroom. Behind her, hanging up to dry, was a line of washing, underwear included. At one point a figure moved behind a door, and a hand briefly appeared on screen.

No problem at all

Mayor Sophie Barker gently suggested that she might want to move her camera, pointing out that everyone could see her washing. Galer was unbothered.

"You can see my washing. You can see me — that's no problem," Galer replied, according to the Otago Daily Times.

And with that she launched into her speech, arguing that councils had wasted money on pet projects while neglecting basic infrastructure.

A fellow councillor later explained that Galer was in Glasgow, Scotland, travelling with another councillor. More than 2,800 people watched the livestream, and the clip was soon picked up by news agencies around the world. Online, viewers were split between those who loved it and those asking why anyone would take a work call from the bathroom.

It is not even the council's first Zoom moment. Back in 2020, a different Dunedin councillor appeared on a video call dusting his office in a suit jacket and, it turned out, bare legs.
