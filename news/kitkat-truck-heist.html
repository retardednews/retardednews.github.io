Thieves Steal 12 Tonnes of KitKats, All 413,793 Bars, and Vanish Without a Trace

Nestlé says the criminals took its slogan too literally. The chocolate is still out there.

By RetardedNews Staff · March 28, 2026 · Italy to Poland, Europe · 2 min read

For decades, KitKat has told the world to have a break. Somewhere in Europe, a gang of thieves decided to have 413,793 of them.

Nestlé announced that a truck carrying about 12 tonnes of KitKat bars had been stolen on its way from the company's production site in central Italy to Poland. The chocolate was meant for shops across Europe. It never arrived.

To make it even more absurd, the stolen bars came from KitKat's new Formula 1 range, shaped like tiny racing cars. The thieves made a clean getaway, which feels appropriate.

In its statement, KitKat said the criminals had taken its famous slogan too literally, and praised their exceptional taste.

When the company went public, neither the truck nor its sweet cargo had been found, and no arrests had been reported. Nobody was hurt.

Nestlé has turned every chocolate fan into a detective. Each bar carries a batch code, and shoppers, shops and wholesalers can scan it to see whether their snack is from the stolen load. If it matches, they get instructions for alerting the company.

The company said the bars are perfectly safe to eat and that it was going public to highlight a real problem: cargo theft is on the rise for businesses of every size. Somewhere, someone is sitting on half a million chocolate racing cars and wondering what to do next.
