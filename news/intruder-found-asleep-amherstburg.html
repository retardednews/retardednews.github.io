Police Rush to a Break-In in Progress. They Find the Intruder Tucked Up in Bed

She took an unusual route into the house. Then she seems to have decided to stay a while.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 6, 2026 · Amherstburg, Ontario, Canada · 2 min read

Just after 8 a.m. on a weekday morning in early November, officers from the Windsor Police Service's Amherstburg detachment were sent to a reported break-in in progress on Victoria Street South.

Amherstburg is a quiet, historic town where the Detroit River meets Lake Erie, about 25 miles south of Windsor. A burglary happening right now is not an everyday call there.

A real-life Goldilocks

Officers believe the intruder got in by taking the cover off an air conditioning unit and climbing through the gap.

Once inside, she does not seem to have gone hunting for valuables. When police went through the house, they found her in one of the beds, fast asleep.

She was arrested without any trouble. The 46-year-old woman was charged with being unlawfully in a dwelling and with mischief under $5,000, and police said she also had an outstanding warrant.

Police did not say how long she had been sleeping by the time they arrived, or whether anyone was home when she climbed in.

The story quickly travelled far beyond Ontario, with TV stations across the United States picking it up.
