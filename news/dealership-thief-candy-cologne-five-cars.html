Thief Steals Five Cars From Dealership, Stops to Eat Candy and Spray the Owner's Cologne

He took a Porsche and a BMW with the For Sale sticker still on it. The BMW's tracker led police to his front door.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 16, 2026 · Hialeah Gardens, Florida, USA · 3 min read

Some thieves are in and out in minutes. Others treat the crime scene like a spa day.

Police in South Florida say 35-year-old Michael Guevara broke into Cabrera's Auto, a dealership in Hialeah Gardens, early on a Sunday morning using a crowbar and a claw hammer. Surveillance video shows him scouting the area first.

Once inside, he grabbed the key fobs and helped himself to five cars worth more than $200,000, including a 2019 Porsche Macan.

Snacks and a spritz

He also found time to eat candy, leaving the wrappers in a BMW, and to spray himself with the owner's cologne.

He drove the cars to various spots and took the BMW home. That was the mistake: police used the car's tracking system to find it at his house, still wearing its For Sale decal.

He smelled great. That is about where the good news ended.

Officers arrested him at gunpoint. He faces burglary, five counts of grand theft and possession of burglary tools, and a judge denied him house arrest. The owner said all five cars were recovered undamaged.
