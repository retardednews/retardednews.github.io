A Woman's Backyard Camera Keeps Catching Coyotes at Her Water Bowl. One of Them Is Not a Coyote

The pack comes back again and again, and the big dark stranger trots in with them every time. Nobody can work out where he came from.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 17, 2026 · Tucson, Arizona, USA · 3 min read

Patricia Suflita Wilson keeps wildlife cameras running in her yard on the west side of Tucson, Arizona, and coyotes coming in for a drink at her water bowl are nothing new. What made her look twice was the animal walking along with them.

It was a dog. A big one, around 60 pounds, which Wilson thinks may be part boxer or part pit bull. It was not hiding, cornered or being chased. It was simply drinking with the pack, as if it belonged there.

A member of the gang

Over the following two weeks the coyotes returned several times, and each time the dog was right there with them. Wilson gave him a name, Sombra, Spanish for shadow, and says everything about the way he behaves suggests the pack has taken him in.

Her first guess was that Sombra was Raven, a dog that had slipped away from Pima Animal Care Center earlier in the fall. The shelter studied her footage and concluded otherwise: the markings on the two dogs don't match. So Sombra's real story, and whether anyone is missing him, is still unknown.

"For this dog, finding a way to trap it and not the coyotes could be very difficult," shelter spokesperson Nikki Reck told AZPM.

For now the shelter says it will only step in if Sombra looks like he is in danger or becomes a risk to people. Wilson, meanwhile, is hunting for a qualified trapper in case his health slips or he falls out with his new family.

Dogs being welcomed into coyote packs is rare, but it has happened before. In Nevada, a dog called Ghost lived with a group of coyotes for several months before people finally managed to bring him in.
