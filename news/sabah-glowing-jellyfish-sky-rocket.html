A Glowing 'Space Jellyfish' Lights Up the Sky Over Sabah. It Was a Chinese Rocket

Pre-dawn videos of a giant luminous cone spread across Malaysia. The space agency had the answer within a day.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 10, 2026 · Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, Malaysia · 2 min read

Early on Monday, November 9, people in Sabah, Malaysia, looked up and saw something that did not belong in the sky: a huge glowing cone, spreading out like a jellyfish.

Videos from around Kota Kinabalu spread quickly on Threads and other platforms, with the usual theories about aliens and secret aircraft. Similar sightings were reported from Sarawak and the Philippines.

Mystery solved

The Malaysian Space Agency, MYSA, said the light came from China's Long March 12 rocket, which launched from Wenchang on Hainan island at 4:24 a.m. Malaysian time.

The effect is known as a space jellyfish. High up, where the air is thin, a rocket's exhaust spreads out widely. The sun, still below the horizon for people on the ground, lights up the plume from below, so it glows against a dark sky.

Not an alien. Just physics, exhaust and very good timing.

It is the same trick that has produced UFO panics around the world after launches. It is also, admittedly, a much better photo than most rockets manage.
