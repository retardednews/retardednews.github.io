Snack Company Unrolls a Single Meat Stick Longer Than a Football Field in Its Parking Lot

It had to be coiled on a giant rolling rack just to get it outside. The makers insist it tastes exactly like the one at the gas station.

By RetardedNews Staff · September 10, 2026 · Omaha, Nebraska, USA · 2 min read

On September 9, several hundred employees of Conagra Brands gathered in a parking lot at the company's campus in Omaha, Nebraska. In front of them was a very long line of white tables. On the tables, a single Slim Jim.

The beef snack stick measured 429 feet 5.4 inches, or about 130.9 metres, longer than an American football field including both end zones. A Guinness World Records adjudicator was there to measure it.

It was certified as the longest meat snack stick ever made. The old record, about 314 feet, had been set by Wenzel's Farm in Wisconsin in 2023. Conagra's version beat it by around 116 feet.

How do you move it?

The project began in June 2025 with a team of 11 employees, according to The Shelby Report, and the finished stick weighed nearly 70 pounds. The team, which called itself the Blaze Crew, made the meat mix in its own kitchen facility. Then it had a problem: how do you dry and carry something that long without it breaking? The answer was to wind it around a large rolling wire rack, which kept the coils apart until the big day.

On record day they wheeled the rack outside and unspooled the stick along the tables. Guinness says the hard part was keeping the shape and texture the same from one end to the other.

"This record-breaking Slim Jim was a true labor of love for our group," said Conagra's Jess Sweley, according to UPI.

The makers say it uses exactly the same recipe and flavour as a normal Slim Jim from a gas station rack. Nobody has said who got to eat it.
