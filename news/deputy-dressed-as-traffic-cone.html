Drivers Kept Ignoring Crosswalks, So a Deputy Turned Herself Into a Giant Orange Traffic Cone

Sixty-seven drivers failed the test anyway. One offered an excuse that was hard to sell.

By RetardedNews Staff · October 27, 2026 · Riverbank, California, USA · 2 min read

In Stanislaus County, in California's Central Valley, the sheriff's office had a problem: too many drivers were not stopping for people on foot. A run of crashes, some of them fatal, had hit pedestrians in crosswalks across the county.

So its motorcycle unit tried something harder to ignore than a leaflet.

Hard to miss

A deputy pulled on a huge orange traffic cone costume and stepped into marked crosswalks in Riverbank and Patterson, always leaving approaching drivers plenty of time to stop. Some did. Others rolled straight past the giant cone, and motorcycle deputies waiting nearby pulled them over.

Over two days, one in each city, deputies issued 67 citations for pedestrian violations and made two arrests for driving under the influence.

The sheriff's office posted video of the stops. In one, a deputy asks a driver whether he really did not notice his partner standing in the middle of the crosswalk dressed as a big orange cone. The driver says he saw her, but could not stop in time.

"You had plenty of opportunity to stop and slow down, partner," the deputy replies.

The operation was paid for by a state traffic safety grant and timed for the darker evenings around Halloween and the end of daylight saving time, when pedestrians are hardest to see.

The sheriff's office urged drivers to stay alert, keep to the speed limit and yield to anyone in a crosswalk, costumed or not.
