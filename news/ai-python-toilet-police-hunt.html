Nine-Year-Old Uses AI to Put a Python in the Toilet. Police Search for Two Days

Firefighters and police in a small Italian town hunted for a snake that only ever existed on a phone.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 14, 2026 · Castelfidardo, Marche, Italy · 3 min read

On November 12, a family in Castelfidardo, a hill town near Ancona in central Italy, called for help. They had a photo of what looked like a royal python poking its head out of their toilet.

Police and firefighters took it seriously. Nobody wants a python loose in the plumbing, so the search began.

It went on for two days. The snake never showed up, because the snake had never existed.

The culprit confesses

The photo had been made by the family's nine-year-old son with an artificial intelligence app. Officers only worked it out after examining the original image on his phone.

The boy owned up and, according to one report, apologised to the officers. Which app he used has not been reported.

Two days, a fire crew and a police unit versus one bored kid. The kid won.

One photography site noted that an AI image detector rated the fake snake as authentic, which says something about how good the tools have become. Castelfidardo's toilets, meanwhile, remain python-free.
