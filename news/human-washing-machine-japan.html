Japan Now Sells a Washing Machine for Humans, and Its Maker Says It Cleans Your Soul

You lie in a pod, close the lid and get washed like a load of laundry. It costs about $385,000.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 28, 2025 · Osaka, Japan · 3 min read

Showers are fine. Baths are nice. But have you ever wanted to be washed exactly like a pair of socks? Japan has you covered.

In late November 2025, the Osaka company Science Co. began selling its Mirai Ningen Sentakki, which translates as Future Human Washing Machine. You lie down inside a pod, close the lid, and it cleans you the way a washing machine cleans clothes. There is, thankfully, no spin cycle.

While you soak, music plays and the machine monitors your heartbeat and other vital signs. A company spokesperson told AFP that it washes not only your body but also your soul.

From Expo novelty to real product

A prototype was one of the hits of Expo 2025 in Osaka, which drew more than 27 million visitors. Demand to try it was so high that people had to win a lottery for a turn.

The idea is not new. It is an update of a human-washing machine shown at the last Osaka Expo in 1970, which inspired the company's president when he was ten years old.

The company only decided to make it for real after a US resort company asked whether it would ever be sold.

Only about 50 units are planned, at a reported price of around ¥60 million, or roughly $385,000 each. An Osaka hotel bought the first one and rents it out in a private spa room, and an electronics chain ordered another to lure shoppers into its stores. Each wash takes about 20 minutes and includes bubbles and a warm mist. Detergent, presumably, is optional.
