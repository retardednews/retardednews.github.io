Hundreds of Timothée Chalamet Clones Swarm a NYC Park, Cops Shut It Down, Then the Real Timothée Shows Up

The prize was $50. The crowd was enormous. The police were not amused. The actor himself was.

By RetardedNews Staff · October 27, 2024 · New York City, USA · 3 min read

It started as a joke. It ended with police, handcuffs, a $500 fine and the arrival of the one man nobody expected to see.

On Sunday, October 27, 2024, a YouTube personality hosted a Timothée Chalamet look-alike contest near Washington Square Park in Manhattan. The prize was a modest $50. Flyers went up around the city, an online invite collected thousands of RSVPs, and hundreds of people turned out to watch.

The contestants came prepared. There were curly-haired Willy Wonkas, desert-ready Dune heroes and a great many sharp cheekbones.

Then the police arrived

Just as the look-alikes started walking a red carpet, police ordered the crowd to break up. Organisers were fined $500 for holding an unpermitted costume contest, and at least one contestant was led away in handcuffs.

Then, out of nowhere, the real Timothée Chalamet walked in, flanked by bodyguards, and posed for photos with his doppelgängers.

Undeterred, the contest moved to a nearby playground. More than a dozen hopefuls took to a makeshift stage, and four finalists were quizzed on their French, their plans to improve the world and their intentions toward Kylie Jenner.

The winner, a 21-year-old from Staten Island in a purple Wonka outfit who threw candy from a briefcase, walked away with a novelty cheque made out to Best Tim. One of the producers summed it up: what began as a silly joke turned into total pandemonium.
