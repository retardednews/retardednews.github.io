Man Drives to Police Station at 2 a.m. to Report Lost Phone, Fails Breath Test in the Parking Lot

Officers noticed his truck before they noticed his complaint. It was on the sidewalk.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 1, 2026 · Abbotsford, British Columbia, Canada · 2 min read

Losing your phone after a night out is annoying. Reporting it to the police is reasonable. Driving there yourself, at 2 a.m., after the bar, is where this story turns.

A 42-year-old man showed up at Abbotsford Police headquarters in British Columbia to report that his phone had gone missing after he left a bar in an Uber.

Before he could get far with the paperwork, a patrol officer spotted his truck being driven on the sidewalk inside the station's own parking lot.

Wrong place, wrong time, wrong everything

Officers gave him a breath test. He failed it.

He was handed a 90-day driving ban and his vehicle was impounded. The Abbotsford Police Department shared the story on social media, where it did considerably better than his night.

He took an Uber home from the bar. Then he drove to the police.

There is no word on whether the phone was ever found. If it was, he will need to get a ride to pick it up.
