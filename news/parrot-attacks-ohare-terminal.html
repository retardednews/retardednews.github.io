A Mystery Bird Flies Into O'Hare and Spends Hours Taking On an Entire Terminal

Travellers thought the green visitor was adorable right up until they reached for it. Then it was one bird against Terminal 5.

By RetardedNews Staff · October 13, 2026 · Chicago, Illinois, USA · 3 min read

Chicago's O'Hare International Airport handles millions of passengers a year. On October 13, one of them came in without a ticket, through an open door, on its own wings.

A large green bird that looked like a parrot flew into Terminal 5 and landed near the Air France check-in line. Nobody knew whose pet it was, and it seemed to have some feathers missing around its neck.

At first it was cute. The bird rode around on one passenger's backpack, then perched on people's heads and luggage while travellers got their phones out.

Then it turned on them

The charm did not last. The hitchhiker began nipping at its host's neck and ears, and from then on, anyone who tried to grab it, coax it or simply be friendly got pecked. For hours, it swooped around the check-in area and avoided every attempt to catch it.

Airport worker Paris Elbert filmed much of the chaos and later told CBS News Chicago that it was funny, crazy and a little sad all at once. People kept trying to help it, she said, and it kept fighting back.

"The bird chose fight, not flight." (Paris Elbert, to CBS News Chicago)

In the end, an airport worker managed to trap the bird in her shirt. It was taken to a vet for a checkup and a scan for a microchip, in the hope of tracking down its owner. It never needed a boarding pass, and it skipped the check-in queue entirely.
