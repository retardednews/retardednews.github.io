Mexico Asks Whether a Pterodactyl Is Flying Over Ciudad Juárez. Experts Say: It Is a Heron

A winged silhouette on a security camera set off dinosaur panic on the border. Officials, scientists and fact-checkers were less excited.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 16, 2026 · Ciudad Juárez, Chihuahua, Mexico · 3 min read

Pterosaurs died out about 66 million years ago. This month, a chunk of the internet decided one had turned up in Ciudad Juárez.

Clips of a large winged shape flapping over the city, apparently caught by a surveillance camera near the Anapra area on the border with El Paso, spread fast. Mexico's best-known ufologist, Jaime Maussan, helped promote them.

Comment sections filled with prehistoric theories. Some people were sure they were looking at a living pterodactyl.

The boring answer

Local Civil Protection officials said it was most likely just a large bird. Specialists pointed to the great blue heron, which can have a wingspan of up to about two metres and looks fairly prehistoric when it flaps.

Snopes rated one widely shared clip as fake and said its source was unclear. Other viewers suspected some of the footage was made with AI.

Not a dinosaur. Just a very big bird having a very good week.

No scientific evidence supports a living pterosaur, in Juárez or anywhere else. The heron has not commented.
