Golden Toilet Thief Admits He Used the £4.75m Loo the Day Before It Vanished. His Review: Splendid

An 18-carat gold toilet was ripped out of Winston Churchill's birthplace in five minutes. It has never been seen again.

By RetardedNews Staff · June 13, 2025 · Oxford, England, UK · 3 min read

It was called America. It was a fully working toilet made of solid 18-carat gold, weighing about 98 kilograms and insured for £4.75 million. And one day it was simply gone.

The toilet, created by Italian artist Maurizio Cattelan, was plumbed in and ready for use as part of an exhibition at Blenheim Palace in Oxfordshire, the birthplace of Winston Churchill. Visitors could book a slot and use it.

Before dawn on September 14, 2019, a gang rammed two stolen vehicles through the palace's locked wooden gates, smashed through a window and a door, and tore the toilet out of the floor. The whole raid took about five minutes.

The world's strangest witness statement

At the trial in 2025, one 39-year-old defendant admitted he had visited the palace the day before the heist and used the golden toilet. Asked in court what the experience was like, he gave a one-word review.

"Splendid."

His phone told a less innocent story. It held photos of the toilet, a selfie with it, a picture of the cubicle lock, and the very window later smashed in the raid. He claimed he had been photographing flags in the distance and simply thought the lock was funny.

The jury was not convinced, and he was convicted of burglary. The ringleader, who had pleaded guilty, got four more years on top of a near 20-year sentence for other crimes. The man who called it splendid got 27 months. The judge called the heist bold and brazen.

None of the gold has ever been found. Prosecutors believe it was cut up and sold, which means somewhere out there, someone may be wearing jewellery made from a toilet.
