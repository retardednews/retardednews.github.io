Steel Conker Scandal: 82-Year-Old King Conker Accused of Cheating With a Metal Chestnut

His opponent's conker shattered in one hit. Then a steel one turned up in the champion's pocket.

By RetardedNews Staff · October 21, 2024 · Southwick, England, UK · 3 min read

Every sport has its doping scandal. The World Conker Championships have a steel chestnut.

More than 200 people entered the 2024 championships, held on October 13 at a pub in the English village of Southwick. The rules are simple: thread a horse chestnut onto a string, take turns whacking your opponent's conker, and try to smash it.

The men's title went to an 82-year-old veteran known to everyone as King Conker. His opponent in the final was less than thrilled, and raised the alarm after his own conker exploded in a single strike.

The smoking chestnut

Organisers confirmed that a steel conker had been found in the champion's pocket. He denied any wrongdoing.

The committee investigated, reviewing photos, video and statements from judges and umpires. On October 21 they cleared him, accepting that he carried the metal conker purely to amuse people as part of his King Conker persona.

The committee's chair said four judges had watched him so closely that cheating was absolutely impossible.

The beaten finalist accepted the verdict gracefully. And in the end, King Conker did not take the overall crown anyway. That went to a 34-year-old woman from Indianapolis, who beat him to become the first American champion since the contest began in 1965.
