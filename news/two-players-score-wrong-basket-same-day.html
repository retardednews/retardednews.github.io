Two College Players Score Into Their Own Basket on the Same Night. One of Their Teams Still Wins

Two different games, two wide-open layups, both at the wrong end, about an hour apart. Only one of the two had a happy ending.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 6, 2026 · Las Cruces, New Mexico, USA · 3 min read

Every basketball player has had the nightmare. You grab the ball, you see an empty hoop, you lay it in, and then the crowd makes a strange noise. On Friday, November 6, it happened for real in two US women's college games, about an hour apart.

The first came early in Northwestern State's game at Arkansas. Under pressure from the Razorbacks' full-court press, junior guard Karma Wynn took a bounce pass from a teammate right under her own basket. Nobody was guarding her. She calmly laid it in. It was the wrong hoop, and it put her side six points behind.

Under the rules a basket scored at the wrong end does not go to the shooter. It counts for the other team, so Arkansas collected two free points. Northwestern State never recovered and lost 89-59.

Twenty seconds to go

The second mistake came at a much worse moment. In Las Cruces, New Mexico State trailed Southern Utah by one with about 20 seconds left. Senior guard Emma Christiano read an inbounds pass perfectly, stole it, and raced in for an uncontested layup.

Again, wrong basket. The home broadcast did not notice at first either. Its play-by-play voice shouted that the Aggies had the lead, until the analyst pointed out that Southern Utah was now three points up.

"Aggies lead!" the play-by-play announcer shouted, according to the AP. They did not.

Here is where the night took a turn. Freshman forward Stacy Chovino scored the Aggies' last two baskets, the second one at the correct end with about two seconds left. New Mexico State won 64-63, its first win of the season.

So two players made the same mistake on the same night. One team lost by 30. The other won by a single point and could laugh about it.
