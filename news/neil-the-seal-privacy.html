Officials Beg Fans to Respect the Privacy of Neil, a 1-Ton Seal Who Fights Parked Cars

He bends bollards, smashes barriers and lies in the road. He also has more TikTok followers than Tasmania has people.

By RetardedNews Staff · July 2, 2026 · Hobart, Tasmania, Australia · 3 min read

Most celebrities ask for privacy after a scandal. Neil has had a dozen scandals, and he is a seal, so officials are asking on his behalf.

Neil is a five-year-old male southern elephant seal weighing around 1,000 kilograms, about as much as a small car. Twice a year, after months of feeding at sea, he hauls himself ashore for a tour of beachside towns in southern Tasmania. His latest visit was his twelfth.

His TikTok following of 1.4 million is more than double Tasmania's entire human population. His criminal record is similarly impressive.

The charge sheet

Over his visits, Neil has bent traffic bollards, damaged a sign warning the public about seals, flattened a fence he tried to vault, picked fights with parked cars and smashed through barriers built specifically to keep him off the roads. He enjoys lying in the middle of the street, occasionally bringing an entire town to a halt, and keeps returning to a favourite sidewalk puddle even after rangers relocate him.

At a news conference in Hobart, a state wildlife official asked fans to give Neil some space, calling his fame a double-edged sword. He warned that people risk loving Neil to death, and asked the public to stop revealing which town the seal is currently in.

A seal scientist explained that young males practise for adult dominance battles. With no rivals around, Neil practises on cars.

Some fans online have embraced him as an anti-authority hero. A local resident called him one of the area's biggest exports. If he reaches adulthood, Neil could grow to about five metres long and triple his weight, which is bad news for the bollards.
