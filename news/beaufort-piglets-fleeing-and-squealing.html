Police in South Carolina Chase Down Two Suspects on Foot. The Charge Sheet Is Something Else

The pair were small, fast and loud. The department's write-up of the arrest is even better than the video.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 5, 2026 · Beaufort, South Carolina, USA · 2 min read

Officers in Beaufort, South Carolina, found themselves in a foot pursuit against two suspects who refused to stop running. Both were very short and very fast.

They were piglets. Two of them, loose on the streets, darting away every time an officer got close.

The paperwork

A witness, Kate Morrison, filmed the chase, and the video was shared by the local tourism page Explore Beaufort. The Beaufort Police Department then posted its own version, with an official-looking summary to go with it.

The suspects were identified as the Bacon Bits. The offence was listed as fleeing and squealing. Police described the pair as career cri-ham-inals.

In the end both piglets were caught safely and handed back to their owner, which the department listed as the case's disposition.

The department's verdict on the whole affair: "No ham was done."
