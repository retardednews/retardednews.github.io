It's Official: A Judge Has Ruled That Tacos and Burritos Are Sandwiches

A strip-mall rule said sandwiches only. A taco shop owner fought it for two years, and won.

By RetardedNews Staff · May 15, 2024 · Fort Wayne, Indiana, USA · 2 min read

The internet has argued for years about whether a hot dog is a sandwich. In Indiana, a court has settled a far bolder question.

The owner of a local restaurant called The Famous Taco wanted to open a second location in a Fort Wayne strip mall. There was one problem. The plaza was bound by an agreement with a neighbouring condo association that allowed only a sandwich-bar-style restaurant selling made-to-order, subway-style sandwiches. No alcohol. No outdoor seating. And, everyone assumed, no tacos.

The rule was reportedly designed to keep national fast-food chains out. When the local plan commission refused to amend it to explicitly allow tacos and burritos, the owner sued.

The ruling heard round the lunch hour

After more than two years of legal back and forth, Allen Superior Court Judge Craig Bobay delivered his verdict in May 2024.

Tacos and burritos, the judge wrote, are Mexican-style sandwiches.

Because the agreement never said the sandwiches had to be American-style, the judge concluded the taco shop had been allowed all along, and the whole fight to amend it had been unnecessary.

The owner said he was relieved and expected the new location to open within a few months. The hot dog question, sadly, remains open.
