A Private Jet Lands at a Quiet Countryside Airfield. It Is Not the Airfield It Was Looking For

The airport the pilots wanted was 11 miles away. The one they picked was home to American bombers, and security was waiting.

By RetardedNews Staff · September 13, 2026 · Fairford, Gloucestershire, England, UK · 3 min read

On the morning of Saturday, September 12, a small business jet took off from London City Airport for a short hop west into the Cotswolds. The aircraft, a German-registered Cessna Citation run by the private jet company VistaJet, was due to land at Cotswold Airport at Kemble, a former RAF field now used by private flyers.

Just over half an hour later it touched down safely on a long runway in the Gloucestershire countryside. The trouble was that it was the wrong runway.

Wrong base, wrong country's air force

The jet had landed at RAF Fairford, about 11 miles east of Kemble. Fairford may carry the Royal Air Force name, but it is run by the United States Air Force and currently hosts a detachment of B-1B Lancer strategic bombers.

An unannounced civilian plane rolling down a bomber runway is not something base security takes lightly. As soon as it touched down, an emergency response kicked in, and plane spotters outside the fence posted pictures of security vehicles gathering around the little jet.

According to the aviation site The Aviationist, the pilot realised the mistake quickly and wanted to leave straight away. Security had other ideas. Nobody was going anywhere until the interviews were done and the paperwork was filled in.

Roughly three hours later the jet was finally allowed to take off again, at about 12:10 p.m. Its flight to the airport it had meant to visit in the first place took about four minutes.

Nobody suggested the landing was anything other than an honest mistake. RAF Fairford did not immediately comment. Similar slip-ups are rare but not unknown: a civilian Pilatus turboprop landed at an RAF base in Wales by mistake in 2020.
