Raccoon Breaks Into Liquor Store, Drinks the Scotch Shelf Dry, Passes Out in the Bathroom

Staff opened up on Saturday morning to smashed bottles, a whisky lake on the floor and one very unconscious masked burglar.

By RetardedNews Staff · December 2, 2025 · Ashland, Virginia, USA · 3 min read

Some people end a long week at the bar. One raccoon in Virginia decided to skip the bar entirely and go straight to the warehouse.

Overnight into Saturday, November 29, the animal broke into a closed, state-run Virginia ABC liquor store near Ashland in Hanover County. Investigators believe it came in through the roof: a ceiling tile gave way, and the raccoon came down with it.

What happened next was less a burglary and more a bender. The raccoon headed straight for the bottom shelf, home of the scotch and whisky, and started knocking bottles over. Glass shattered. Liquor pooled across the floor. Security cameras captured the aftermath, which looked roughly like a frat party with fewer witnesses.

The county's animal control officer said the raccoon had drunk nearly everything it could reach.

When an employee arrived the next morning, the culprit was not hiding or fleeing. It was lying flat out on the bathroom floor, completely unconscious.

Hanover County Animal Protection and Shelter collected the suspect and, in a rare act of mercy toward a burglar, simply let it sleep it off. After a few hours of rest it had no injuries at all, except what staff described as a probable hangover.

The shelter later joked online about the raccoon's poor life choices and said it hoped the animal had learned that breaking and entering is wrong. Once sober, the raccoon was released back into the wild with no charges filed and, presumably, a pounding headache.
