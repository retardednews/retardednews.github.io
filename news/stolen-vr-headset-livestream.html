Burglar Breaks In and Steals Just One Thing: A Kid's VR Headset. Then He Puts It On

The family had barely unpacked. A few hours later they were watching the thief from their own phone.

By RetardedNews Staff · October 6, 2026 · Ocoee, Florida, USA · 3 min read

A family in Ocoee, a suburb west of Orlando, had only just moved into their new house when someone crept in overnight through the sliding glass door at the back. There were boxes everywhere, but the intruder ignored nearly all of them.

He left with a single item: the children's virtual reality headset.

The feed nobody switched off

What he could not have known was that the headset was still paired with one of the parents' phones. The family liked to watch their kids' games through a live stream on their mobile, and that link was still active.

So the moment the burglar switched on his new toy, the family, and soon the police, had a front-row view of whatever he was looking at. The picture showed the inside of a parked car and, beyond it, a house with a very distinctive green roof.

The headset has no GPS, so the roof was the only real clue. Luckily, Ocoee Police Sergeant Kevin Birdsong had grown up in the area and recognised it.

Within about an hour, officers were knocking on the door of that house. The man inside handed the headset over.

Police identified him as 18-year-old Ja'Mir Edmond Baker. He was taken to the Orange County jail on an existing warrant, which reports say concerned an iPad allegedly taken from a parked car months earlier. The headset went back to the kids.

Experts quoted after the case had simple advice for everyone else: leave tracking and remote monitoring switched on, and lock the sliding door.
