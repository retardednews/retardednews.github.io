Woman Empties a Halloween Candy Bowl on Camera, Gets Fired for Lying About Saying Sorry

She was wearing her work shirt. Her sushi restaurant boss was more upset about the fake apology than the candy.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 3, 2026 · Helena, Alabama, USA · 3 min read

Leaving out a bowl of candy on Halloween is an act of trust. In Helena, Alabama, a doorbell camera showed that trust being scooped up by the handful.

The video shows two adult women at an unattended candy bowl. One of them took nearly everything in it, while wearing a shirt from Sakana Sushi Bar & Grill.

The clip spread on Facebook, and the internet did what it does: it read the shirt.

The cover-up was worse than the crime

The restaurant confirmed that the woman was an off-duty employee. At first, it said she had gone back, returned the candy and apologised.

The homeowner disagreed. He said the only apology he got came from the other woman in the video. Sakana then said it had confirmed the employee lied about apologising, and fired her.

Taking candy from children: bad. Lying to your boss about it: unemployment.

The restaurant said it does not condone taking from children or hurting the community. Alabama is an at-will employment state, so the candy may end up being the most expensive she ever ate.
