Woman Feeds Her Dogs Invisible Treats. One Catches On. The Other Is Still Chewing

Xena figured it out by round two and walked away. Luna kept happily eating nothing.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 17, 2026 · Online (YouTube Shorts) · 2 min read

Not every viral story needs a crime. Some just need a dog with total faith in humanity.

In a YouTube Short that spread this week, a woman gives her two dogs, Xena and Luna, a real treat. Then she quietly switches to pretend ones to see how long it takes them to notice.

At first, both dogs happily accept the imaginary snacks.

The great treat experiment

By the second round, Xena has worked it out. She gives her owner a look and walks off.

Luna does not walk off. Luna keeps taking every invisible treat offered, chewing and swallowing with complete commitment.

Xena wants a treat. Luna simply enjoys the idea of one.

Commenters agreed that Luna is living her best life, and that the real treat is never questioning anything. The clip was posted by the account @heymynamesluna.
