Passengers Wait Two Hours for Their Bags, Then They Roll Out Smeared in Brown Goo. The Airline Says It's Not What It Looks Like

Travellers at Atlanta's airport assumed the worst. American Airlines has another explanation, and not everyone is buying it.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 16, 2026 · Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, Georgia, USA · 3 min read

American Airlines Flight 2609 from Miami landed in Atlanta at about 8:30 p.m. on Monday, November 16. For the passengers, the flight turned out to be the easy bit.

They say they then spent more than two hours at carousel 8 waiting for their luggage. When it finally came round, dozens of suitcases were coated in a sticky brown substance.

Passenger Derick McCallum filmed the moment for TikTok. The clip captures the crowd recoiling, and one traveller announcing what everyone was thinking: the bags were covered in, to use the technical term, doo-doo.

The official verdict

American Airlines says no. In a statement to Atlanta TV station WSB, the airline said the mess was peanut butter that had leaked out of one passenger's checked bag during the flight and spread onto everyone else's luggage.

The airline apologised and promised to work directly with the affected customers to put things right. In the meantime, passengers were left scrubbing their own suitcases with disinfecting wipes and tissues in the middle of baggage claim.

The doubters

McCallum is not convinced. His problem is the sheer quantity involved.

"How much peanut butter does somebody pack to cover that many bags?" (Derick McCallum, via WSB-TV)

Nobody has answered that publicly. Peanut butter is allowed in checked luggage, for what it's worth. The airline has not said whose bag it was, how big the jar was, or whether its owner made it home with enough left for breakfast.
