Man Spends 8 Years Gluing 706,000 Matchsticks Into a Giant Eiffel Tower. Guinness Says He Used the Wrong Matches

The record was rejected over a technicality. Then the internet got angry and Guinness backed down.

By RetardedNews Staff · February 9, 2024 · Montpellier-de-Médillan, France · 3 min read

Some people build model ships in bottles. One Frenchman built an Eiffel Tower taller than a two-storey house, out of matchsticks.

The 47-year-old spent eight years and about 4,200 hours on his creation, a 1:45 scale replica standing 7.19 metres tall. It used more than 706,000 matchsticks and 23 kilograms of glue. He placed the final match on December 27, 2023.

Early on, he cut the flammable heads off ordinary matches by hand. When that became too tedious, he started ordering plain wooden sticks without heads directly from a French match manufacturer. That decision would come back to haunt him.

Rejected

In late January 2024, Guinness World Records turned down his record attempt. The reason: his sticks were not commercially available matches.

He told French television the rejection had really hurt. The public agreed it was absurd, and after a wave of criticism, Guinness reversed its decision.

A Guinness records director admitted the organisation had been heavy-handed and said it regretted the distress it had caused.

Guinness also rewrote its rules so that future builders can cut and shape their matchsticks however they like. The Frenchman's tower officially beat the previous record of 6.53 metres, set in 2009. He even hired a survey company to confirm the height, just in case anyone else had questions.
