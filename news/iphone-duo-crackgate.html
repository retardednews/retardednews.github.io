Apple Finally Sells a Folding iPhone for $1,999. Within Days, Owners Start Posting Photos of the Fold

Apple calls the early response 'incredibly positive'. The internet has already given the problem a nickname.

By RetardedNews Staff · October 26, 2026 · Cupertino, California, USA · 3 min read

Samsung sold its first folding phone back in 2019. Apple took its time, and its first foldable, the iPhone Duo, went on sale only last week, with a 7.6-inch inner screen that opens like a book and a price tag of $1,999.

Within days, owners on Reddit and other forums began posting close-up photos of that inner screen. The pictures looked worryingly alike: a crack or a set of white lines running straight down the middle, right along the hinge.

Some said it happened within hours of unboxing. One owner said their screen simply gave out overnight. People started calling it 'crackgate', and the jokes wrote themselves, including one Reddit poster who said the phone had more crack than a plumber.

What Apple says

Apple told reporters it is "aware of an issue impacting the inner display on a small number of iPhone Duo units" and asked affected owners to contact AppleCare.

It added that most phones are not at risk, and that owners do not need to have paid for AppleCare+ to get a replacement. Apple has not said what is causing the problem.

Others have been guessing. A teardown by repair site iFixit pointed to parts that may press too hard on the screen, independent tests found dust can sneak into the hinge despite the phone's dust rating, and one YouTube analysis suggested the hinge has a weak spot.

There is one more twist. 9to5Mac, which tracked the complaints, noticed that some of the cracked-screen posts flooding Reddit appear to be fake, AI-generated images. Several are real, though, which leaves Apple's newest phone with a problem in exactly the place it was supposed to shine.
