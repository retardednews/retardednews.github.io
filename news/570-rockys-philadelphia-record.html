Hundreds of Rocky Balboas Storm the Philadelphia Museum Steps. Not All of Them Were Counted

Grey sweats, black beanie, black high-tops. Guinness had a strict dress code, and a judge was checking.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 13, 2026 · Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA · 3 min read

Fifty years after Rocky first ran up the steps of the Philadelphia Museum of Art, the city threw a month of parties for its favourite fictional boxer. One of them was a world record attempt: the largest gathering of people dressed as Rocky Balboa.

On Friday, November 13, in cold drizzle, fans in identical outfits packed the famous steps. Guinness World Records needed at least 250 of them for the record to count.

There were rules. To be counted you had to wear a grey hooded sweatshirt and grey sweatpants, a black beanie and black Converse high-tops, the look Sylvester Stallone wore in his training montage. Some people got free costumes from the organisers. Others put theirs together from Amazon and Walmart.

The count

A Guinness adjudicator walked the crowd before giving a number, and a handful of would-be Rockys were taken off the total because their outfits did not meet the rules. Even so, the final figure was well over double the minimum.

"Philadelphia, today you achieved 570," adjudicator Andy Glass told the crowd, according to Billy Penn.

Once the record was official, the 570 Rockys did the only thing that made sense. They ran up the steps together and posed around the Rocky statue for a group photo.

Fans came from all over. A former wrestler from Allentown wrapped his hands in athletic tape from his school days. A couple flew in from Florida after failing to get an online registration and simply bought the pieces themselves. Several people said the same thing: everyone loves an underdog.

The weather did win one round. Cold and rain forced the city to postpone a rededication of the statue and cancel a Philly Pops concert. The record attempt went ahead anyway, which is very on-brand for Rocky.
