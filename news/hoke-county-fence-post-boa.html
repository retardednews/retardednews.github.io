Farmer Fixing His Fence Spots a Fence Post Lying in the Grass. He Doesn't Remember Dropping It

It was about seven feet long and in roughly the right place. Then he took a closer look.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 6, 2026 · Hoke County, North Carolina, USA · 2 min read

In early October, the animal shelter in Hoke County, North Carolina, put out a warning: a pet boa constrictor, six to seven feet long, had escaped from a home in the Calloway Road area near Raeford. Residents were told to keep their distance and call animal control if they saw it.

Weeks went by with no sign of it. Richard Wood, who farms four doors down from where the snake got out, admits he had more or less forgotten about it.

A post that moved

Then one Wednesday he was out repairing a fence with his daughter, Peyton, and noticed what looked like a fence post on the ground. He assumed one of the workers had dropped it. It turned out to be the missing boa, almost a month after it vanished.

Animal control said it would take about half an hour to get there, and Wood was worried the snake would slip off again in the meantime. So he called a friend who keeps large snakes and asked what to do. Picking it up himself, he decided, was not an option.

"Peyton got it by the tail. I got it around by his head. He went right in," Wood told WNCN.

"In" meant a Rubbermaid storage tub. Officers arrived with Matthew George, known online as Mountain Matt, who took the snake into his care.

It turns out the owner had moved to Wilmington and left the boa with his father, and it escaped after its enclosure was moved into an outdoor building. Wood, for his part, thinks the snake was glad the adventure was over. It is staying with Mountain Matt until its owner collects it.
