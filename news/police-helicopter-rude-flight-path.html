Police Helicopter Wakes a Toddler at 11 p.m. Her Dad Checks the Flight Tracker and Finds a Very Rude Drawing

The aircraft circled a Hampshire estate for ages. Once the route showed up online, the police had some explaining to do.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 2, 2026 · Fleet, Hampshire, England · 3 min read

Gordon Bird, 53, lives on the Ancells Farm estate in Fleet, Hampshire. At around 11 p.m. on Monday, November 2, a police helicopter started circling over the houses and kept at it for at least 15 minutes. The racket woke his two-year-old daughter.

Bird says his house shakes and the windows rattle when helicopters pass over. He was still up with his computer on, so he did what any curious, slightly irritated parent would do and looked the aircraft up on a public flight-tracking site.

The picture in the sky

The route on his screen was not a tidy search pattern. Traced mostly above nearby Blackbushe Airport before the helicopter looped back over his estate, the track formed an unmistakable outline of a penis, with all the accompanying anatomy.

By his account, the buzzing over the houses lasted 15 to 20 minutes, the drawing itself took around half an hour, and the full flight ran for about an hour before the helicopter went home to its base at Redhill.

The official explanation

The National Police Air Service, which flies helicopters for forces across England and Wales, says the artwork was a coincidence. The crew was helping Thames Valley Police hunt for a vehicle that had failed to stop, circling the roads and junctions officers on the ground sent it to. Its flights, the service said, never follow set routes because they have to react to events as they unfold.

In an email to Bird, the service said it understood his frustration and called it "unfortunate" that the path on tracking apps looked like a particular shape.

Bird is taking it in his stride. He told the BBC he was not angry, only upset about the waste of resources. Whether the crew ever found the car has not been reported. What the flight trackers found is now all over the internet.
