British Angler Lands a 52kg Carp Called Angelina and Declares Himself a World Record Holder

The fish weighed as much as a small adult. Whether it is officially a world record is a different argument.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 4, 2026 · Balatonederics, Hungary · 3 min read

Most fishing stories involve the one that got away. Ash Hegarty's involves the one that barely fit in the net.

On October 28, the 32-year-old from Blackburn, England, hooked a common carp at Euro Aqua, a private fishing lake near Balatonederics in Hungary. It weighed 114lb 10oz, roughly 52kg.

The fish already had a name: Angelina. Hegarty said she looked like a car tyre in the net.

Record, or not?

Hegarty shared the catch on Facebook, calling himself a world record holder, and hundreds of people congratulated him.

Others were less impressed. Critics questioned whether fish from private, well-fed lakes should count, and there has been no confirmed formal international verification of the record.

She weighs more than a lot of people reading this. Her name is Angelina.

The record books also treat common and mirror carp differently. Angelina is about 800g heavier than a famous 2018 mirror carp, and Euro Aqua has form: a Polish angler caught a 47.4kg carp there in 2024.
