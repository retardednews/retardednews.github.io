A 'Jellyfish UFO' With Tentacles Was Filmed Near an Alaska Air Base. The Leading Theory: A Kite

The internet saw aliens. A UFO filmmaker and a former Pentagon UFO investigator both saw an octopus-shaped show kite.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 14, 2026 · Anchorage, Alaska, USA · 3 min read

On June 15, photographer Sara Jane Osborn filmed something odd in Anchorage, Alaska: a dark, jellyfish-like object that seemed to hover above a distant tree line, tentacles and all.

After about three minutes, it was gone. The footage resurfaced online in November and took off, helped by one detail: the tree line sits in front of Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson, home to F-22 fighter jets.

There is no verified evidence the object entered the base's airspace or had anything to do with the military. That did not slow the alien theories down.

Enter the kite

Then the explanations arrived. UFO filmmaker Jeremy Corbell, who has promoted a famous jellyfish-shaped object filmed over Iraq, reportedly concluded on November 11 that this one was most likely a tethered octopus-style show kite.

Former Pentagon UFO investigator Sean Kirkpatrick also reportedly favoured a kite. Other experts suggested high-altitude balloons or military exercises.

When the UFO guys say kite, it is probably a kite.

Osborn is not convinced. She said she knows the colourful kites flown in Anchorage, though she accepted it might have been a drone-like object. Officially, it remains unidentified.
