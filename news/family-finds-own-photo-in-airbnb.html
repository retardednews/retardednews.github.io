Sisters Walk Into Their Airbnb and Find a Framed Photo of Their Own Family on the Wall

The beach print was taken a decade earlier by a stranger with a camera. Commenters say they have seen it in rentals elsewhere too.

By RetardedNews Staff · July 20, 2026 · Oceanside, California, USA · 3 min read

Vacation rentals are full of generic beach art. Usually it does not include you.

Sisters Libby and Aubrey Birrell, from Utah, were staying at a rental in Oceanside, near San Diego, with about 30 relatives to celebrate their grandfather's 80th birthday when they noticed the big aerial beach print on the wall.

In it were their dad, Libby, and their brother Brady, on a beach outing roughly ten years earlier.

How did they get there?

Aubrey posted the discovery on TikTok and it went viral. The host said the home's decorator was a local photographer who takes aerial beach shots and sells them as large prints to vacation rentals.

The sisters think the photo was taken at Mission Beach or Imperial Beach, during a family trip for their brother's soccer tournament. The host offered them a copy.

You came for the beach. The beach had already come for you.

Not everyone thinks the mystery is fully solved, and the story kicked off a debate about strangers' photos used as decor. Some commenters claimed to have seen the same print in rentals in other states, which has not been confirmed.
