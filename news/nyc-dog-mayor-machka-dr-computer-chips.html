A Three-Legged Earthquake Survivor Wins New York's Dog Mayor Race by 288 Votes. Her Deputy Is Called Dr. Computer Chips

She survived an earthquake, lost a leg and crossed an ocean before winning a 16-candidate race. Then she celebrated with ice cream.

By RetardedNews Staff · November 10, 2026 · Sunnyside, Queens, New York City, USA · 3 min read

Move over, Mayor Mamdani. New York City has a second mayor, and she won by 288 votes after a gruelling 15-round contest.

Her name is Machka. She lives in Sunnyside, Queens, she has three legs, and she is an Anatolian Shepherd mix. She is now the city's third Honorary Dog Mayor.

How the race works

Stephen Calabria, a New York media producer, launched the NYC Dog Mayor election in 2022 as a bit of joy for a city going through hard times. It follows the human political calendar, with a vote every two years. Sixteen dogs enter a March Madness-style bracket and the public votes them through 15 rounds.

In the final, Machka won 4,877 votes. The runner-up, a 13-year-old rescue Shih Tzu who goes by Dr. Computer Chips, got 4,589. In a fine show of bipartisanship, he will serve as Deputy Dog Mayor.

A campaign story with real bite

Machka's life story would suit any human candidate. She was born on the streets of Elbistan, Turkey, and lost a leg when a car hit her as a puppy. The woman who took her in then lost her home in the 2023 earthquake. Machka eventually reached New York, where Kylen Button adopted her in 2025 through a rescue group.

She ran on an actual platform, too: more pet-friendly housing and homeless shelters. Button, who doubled as campaign manager, says the issue is personal, because many shelters turn away people with pets, which can stop them from getting help.

The official website describes the job as part ambassador and part professional sniffer. Machka is the first dog mayor from Queens, following Sally Long Dog and Simon, a basset and cattle dog mix. Neighbours swarmed her once the result was official, and she marked the win with ice cream. According to Button, Machka would happily campaign for the rest of her life. Her inauguration is set for January 17, 2027.
