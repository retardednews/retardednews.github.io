Orcas Bring Back 1987's Hottest Fashion Trend: Wearing Dead Salmon as Hats

A killer whale was photographed with a fish balanced on its head, reviving a mystery that has baffled scientists for nearly 40 years.

By RetardedNews Staff · December 4, 2024 · Puget Sound, Washington, USA · 3 min read

Fashion is cyclical, and apparently that applies to killer whales too.

On October 25, 2024, a photographer near Point No Point in Puget Sound captured a male orca swimming along with a dead salmon perched neatly on top of his head. The nonprofit Orca Network reported the sighting, and researchers identified the whale as J27, nicknamed Blackberry, a 32-year-old male from J pod.

J pod belongs to the endangered southern resident killer whales, a population of about 71 animals split into three pods that eat nothing but fish. And they have a history with this particular look.

The summer of the salmon hat

In 1987, a female in K pod started wearing salmon on her head. Within months, the trend had spread to the other two pods. Then, almost as suddenly as it began, it vanished, and it was barely seen again for decades.

The new sighting came during an unusually big chum salmon run, the same week J pod spent a rare ten days in a row being spotted in the area. That may matter, because nobody actually knows why orcas do this. Theories include playing with spare food, saving a snack for later, or some kind of social food-sharing.

One biologist summed up the science honestly: it is all speculation.

She has seen southern residents wear the look four or five times since 2005, and says it at least suggests a whale with plenty to eat. Other experts are not ready to call a full comeback. One photo, they note, does not make a trend. Fashion critics in the Pacific Northwest remain divided.
