Couple Accidentally Mails Their Cat to Amazon in a Box of Boots. She Survives 6 Days and 600 Miles

The returns warehouse opened the package expecting steel-toed boots. It found a calico instead.

By RetardedNews Staff · April 24, 2024 · Lehi, Utah to Jurupa Valley, California, USA · 3 min read

Every cat owner knows cats love boxes. One family in Utah learned exactly how far that love can take a cat: about 630 miles.

On April 10, 2024, a man in Lehi, Utah, packed five pairs of steel-toed work boots back into a large box to return them to Amazon. What he did not notice was Galena, the family's six-year-old indoor calico and a well-known box enthusiast, who had quietly climbed in with them.

The box was sealed and shipped. The family spent the next week searching for their cat, putting up posters and posting appeals on social media, with no idea she was riding UPS across state lines.

Return to sender

On April 16, a worker at an Amazon returns warehouse in Jurupa Valley, California, opened the box and found a cat where the boots should have been. An off-duty employee who rescues cats on the side came in with food and a carrier and took her home.

Galena had spent roughly six days in the box with no food or water. A vet found her a little thinner and mildly dehydrated but otherwise fine, with normal bloodwork. Her microchip led the vet straight to her owners, who at first assumed the phone call was a prank.

The owners credited three small miracles: a split seam that let in air, mild weather, and the warehouse worker who took her in.

The couple flew to California for the reunion, then drove about ten hours home with Galena. Their advice to other pet owners: get your pets microchipped, and triple-check your Amazon boxes.
