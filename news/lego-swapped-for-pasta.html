Man Accused of Swapping $34,000 of LEGO for Pasta. Police Say His Plan Got Cooked Al Dente

He allegedly bought LEGO sets, kept the pieces, filled the boxes with dried pasta and returned them. At least 70 times.

By RetardedNews Staff · April 16, 2026 · Irvine, California, USA · 2 min read

Some people build LEGO. One man allegedly built a pasta-based retail scheme so elaborate it deserves its own instruction booklet.

Police in Irvine, California, arrested a 28-year-old man from nearby Paramount on suspicion of grand theft. According to investigators, his method was simple. Buy expensive LEGO sets at Target. Take out the minifigures and valuable pieces. Fill the boxes back up with dried pasta so they weigh about the same. Return them for a full refund.

Police say he pulled it off at least 70 times at Target stores across the country, racking up around $34,000 in losses.

A bad build

Irvine detectives eventually set up surveillance at a local Target, identified their man and arrested him at the store, all captured on officers' body cameras.

The department then released photos of the recovered evidence: piles of LEGO figures, including Star Wars characters, alongside bags of durum wheat semolina pasta allegedly used as stand-ins.

On Facebook, police called it a bad build and warned that any master plan swapping LEGOs for linguine will end up cooked al dente.

He was booked into Orange County Jail. Target has not said whether it plans to start weighing its returns more carefully, or simply start checking them for spaghetti.
