Rhode Island Man Grows a 3,156-Pound Pumpkin, the First Ever to Break 3,000

It beat a world record that had stood for exactly one week, by nearly 300 pounds.

By RetardedNews Staff · October 10, 2026 · Warren, Rhode Island, USA · 3 min read

A small car weighs about 3,000 pounds. Ron Wallace grew a pumpkin that weighs more.

At the Southern New England Giant Pumpkin Weigh Off at Frerichs Farm in Warren, Rhode Island, on October 10, Wallace's pumpkin hit the scale at 3,156 pounds, or 1,431.5kg.

Guinness World Records confirmed it as the heaviest pumpkin ever, and the first in history to pass the 3,000-pound mark.

A record that lasted a week

The previous record had been set only a week earlier, on October 3, by British grower Ian Paton with a pumpkin of about 2,857 pounds. Wallace beat it by nearly 300.

This is not his first giant. Wallace, 60, set a record in 2006 with a 1,502-pound pumpkin and in 2012 became the first grower to break 2,000 pounds. This is his third world record.

One pumpkin. Heavier than a car. Grown on purpose.

His secret this year? It was his first season growing in a greenhouse. Guinness called the double record-break 'Pumpkinpalooza'.
