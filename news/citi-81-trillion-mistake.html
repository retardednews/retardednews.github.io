Bank Accidentally Puts $81 Trillion Into a Customer's Account Instead of $280

One employee typed it. A second checked it. Neither noticed the extra 11 zeros.

By RetardedNews Staff · February 28, 2025 · New York, USA · 2 min read

Everyone has made a typo at work. Few have made one worth almost three times the entire US economy.

In April 2024, Citigroup credited a customer account with $81 trillion. The amount was supposed to be $280. The Financial Times revealed the blunder in February 2025, and other outlets including CBS News followed up.

Here is the part that makes it truly special. The payment was entered by one employee and then reviewed by a second. Neither spotted the problem, and the transaction was approved to go through the next day.

Caught by employee number three

About 90 minutes after it posted, a third employee noticed something slightly off about an $81 trillion balance. The transaction was reversed a few hours later.

Citi said no money ever actually left the bank, describing the cause as an inputting error between two of its own internal ledger accounts. It reported the incident to US regulators as a near miss, the term for a wrong payment that gets caught and recovered.

According to an internal report cited by the FT, Citi had 10 near misses of $1 billion or more in 2024. That was an improvement on the year before.

For perspective, the entire US economy produced about $29 trillion in 2024. For a few hours, one account at Citi held nearly three of them.
