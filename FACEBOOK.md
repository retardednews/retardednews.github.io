# Facebook post rules

## Goal
Make every post as clickbaity as possible. The reader should have to click the link to find out what happened.

## How
- Tease the story but hold back the payoff. Never give away the ending, the twist or the punchline in the post.
- Open with the strangest detail, then stop right before the explanation.
- Curiosity-gap phrasing works well: "Police thought it was a robbery. It wasn't.", "Nobody expected what was inside the box."
- Keep it short: 1–2 lines, then the link.
- Use the story's best picture as the image, cropped so it raises a question.

## Schedule
- One post every 4 hours, 24/7: 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 local (UTC+2) — 6 posts a day.
- Schedule in Meta Business Suite Planner. Fill the next empty slot; never stack two posts in one slot.
- Start right away: when a new batch of posts is approved, publish the first one immediately (Publish, not Schedule), then schedule the rest one per 4-hour slot after it.
- No gaps: the page should never go more than 4 hours without a post. Keep the queue booked at least 2 days ahead, and when it's about to run out, add new stories and posts.
- Never boost a post (paid ad) — dismiss the Boost pop-up with "Maybe later".

## Limits
- The tease must match the article. Exaggerate the mystery, not the facts. A misleading hook gets the page flagged.
- No engagement bait like "Comment YES", "Tag a friend" or "Share if you agree". Facebook demotes posts that ask for reactions or shares.
